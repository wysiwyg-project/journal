Together with filmmakers Mahdi Hosseingholi and Mojtaba Zarghampour, Sandra Bischler-Hartmann travelled to Ticino to film an interview with graphic designer Polly Bertram. Bertram presented various works from her private archive, including some of her first experiments with the Macintosh in the early 1990s, and shared her views on the role of computers in graphic design practice.
