Sandra Bischler-Hartmann conducted an interview with Michael Renner, graphic designer and head of the Institute Digital Communication Environments at the Basel Academy of Art and Design. Filmed by Thibéry Maillard, the interview featured Renner presenting various projects from his archive, including his first experiments with the Macintosh as a student in 1984, his graphic design work for Apple Computer, Inc. in California in the late 1980s, and materials from his courses as a teacher of computer-based graphic design at the Basel School of Design in the early 1990s.
